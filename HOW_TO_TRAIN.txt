📊 WHAT THE SCRIPT DOES
================================================================================

Step 1: Combines ALL Datasets (30 seconds)
   - Copies PlantVillage unified data
   - Merges Indian crops data
   - Removes duplicates
   - Shows statistics

Step 2: Loads Data (1-2 minutes)
   - Creates train/validation split (85/15)

Step 3: Builds Model (10 seconds)

   - Custom classification head
