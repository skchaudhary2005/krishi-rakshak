CHALLENGES:

   - Both are essentially the same disease
   - Model correctly identifies the disease, just picks one variant

⚠️  Some visually similar diseases confused
   - Tomato Early Blight vs Pepper Bacterial Spot (similar leaf patterns)
   - This is a known challenge even for human experts
